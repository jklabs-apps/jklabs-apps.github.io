AudiFi Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

AudiFi ("the app") is an independent music player that allows users to discover and stream music through the Audius platform, listen to locally stored music, and use music playback and library features.

This Privacy Policy explains how AudiFi handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

AudiFi does not require you to create an account with us to use its core features. We do not operate our own user-account servers or maintain a database of users' personal information.

AudiFi does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location for advertising or profiling purposes.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Certain information is stored locally on your device to provide application functionality. This may include:

  • App preferences and settings
  • Music library information and favorites
  • Playlist and playback-related information
  • Downloaded music available for offline playback
  • Information associated with imported local music
  • Other data necessary for the app's features

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to a server operated by the AudiFi developer.

Removing AudiFi from your device may delete locally stored app information and downloaded content.

3. Audius Platform and Network Requests

AudiFi accesses music-related information and streaming content through third-party services associated with the Audius platform. When you use features such as music discovery, search, or streaming, your device connects to external services to retrieve the requested content.

These third-party services may receive technical information associated with network requests, such as your IP address, request information, and other information processed by their services.

AudiFi does not control the privacy practices of these third-party services. Their collection and use of information are governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

Audius: https://audius.org/

4. Music Downloads and Local Music

AudiFi may allow you to download supported music for offline listening and import music files available on your device.

Downloaded music and imported local audio files are stored on your device. AudiFi does not upload your personal music files to servers operated by the developer.

5. Permissions

AudiFi may request device permissions when necessary to provide specific features, such as accessing or importing audio files, downloading content, or providing playback functionality.

Permissions are used for their intended application functionality. AudiFi does not use device permissions to collect personal information for advertising or profiling.

6. Analytics and Advertising

AudiFi does not include developer-operated advertising or use personal information for targeted advertising.

AudiFi does not intentionally use third-party analytics services to track users' behavior across applications or websites.

7. Data Sharing

AudiFi does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.

Network requests necessary for music discovery, searching, streaming, or other features may be processed by the third-party services required to provide those features.

8. Data Security

AudiFi is designed to keep application-related user data locally on your device. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and any backups you create.

Although reasonable measures are taken to protect the app and its data, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

AudiFi is not specifically designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the application, its features, or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
